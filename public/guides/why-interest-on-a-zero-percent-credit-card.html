Why am I paying interest on my 0% credit card?

Check which balance your 0% offer covers and when it ends. A card can list a promotional rate for a balance transfer and a separate rate for purchases or cash transactions.

Read the offer alongside the statement

  1. Find the offer type. Look for a purchase or balance transfer promotion and match it to the balance category shown.
  2. Check the expiry. Compare the offer end date with the period covered by the statement. If the expiry isn’t shown, check the original offer or ask the provider.
  3. Read the charge label. Check whether the amount is listed as interest or as a fee. A transfer fee and an interest charge are separate entries.

An example

Your statement lists a £900 transfer at 0% and a separate standard purchase rate. The 0% line only tells you about the transfer. If the statement shows an interest charge without a breakdown, ask the provider which balance generated it.
