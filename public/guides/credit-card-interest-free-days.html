Understanding your statement

Do you really get 56 interest-free days on a credit card?

Some UK cards offer up to 56 days interest-free on qualifying purchases. The number of days depends on when you buy, which statement includes the purchase and when that statement is due.

In this example, the 11 May purchase lands just after the previous statement closes. It has 29 more days before payment is due than the 9 June purchase, which lands just before the next statement closes.

Check what you need to pay to qualify

Look at your card’s purchase interest terms and the due date on the statement that includes the purchase. You generally need to pay the balance your issuer requires in full by that date. Some issuers, including Halifax, also check whether you met their payment conditions in the previous statement period. Cash withdrawals and promotional balances can follow different rules.
