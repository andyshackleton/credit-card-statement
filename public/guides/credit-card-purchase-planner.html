What could a big purchase cost on your credit card?

Planning a holiday or another large purchase? See how long it could take to repay and what interest could add to the price. Start with the example or enter your own figures.

This estimate assumes interest applies from the first month, a fixed rate and payment, no existing balance, and no new spending or fees. It adds interest monthly. Your provider’s actual charges can vary with payment dates and card terms.

Look at the cost as well as the monthly payment

Try £200 and £300 a month on the same £2,000 holiday. Compare the total interest and the time each payment would take to clear the balance. The final payment can be smaller because the planner stops once the balance reaches zero.

Check your statement before using the estimate

Your statement shows the minimum payment and due date. Use those figures to check any payment you model. If your purchase qualifies for an interest-free period and you pay the required balance in full by the due date, the purchase may cost less than this estimate.
